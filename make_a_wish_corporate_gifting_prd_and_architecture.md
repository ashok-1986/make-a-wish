# Make A Wish — Year-Round Corporate Gifting Website PRD & Scalable Architecture

- **Project Name:** Make A Wish — 365-Day B2B Corporate Gifting & Bundled Solutions Portal
- **Document Version:** 3.0 (Post-Diwali Year-Round Corporate Narrative + Attached Product Catalogues + Zero-Backend Extensible Architecture)
- **Primary Objective:** B2B Lead Generation via Web Inquiry Forms & Direct WhatsApp Routing (`+91 97734 11805`)
- **Commerce Model:** Price-Free B2B Showcase (No Public Pricing, No On-Site Customization, No E-Commerce Checkout)
- **Service Region:** Navi Mumbai (Panvel, Kharghar, Belapur, Nerul, Vashi, Airoli), Thane & Greater Mumbai

# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Purpose & Post-Diwali Narrative Shift
With the Diwali festive window concluding, the **Make A Wish** website narrative shifts from seasonal festival gifting to a **365-Day B2B Corporate Gifting Flagship for Corporates**. Corporates procure gifts year-round for **employee onboarding (joining kits), annual town halls, sales conferences, New Year 2027 desk rollouts, client appreciation, leadership milestones, wellness initiatives, and corporate event celebrations**.

The platform showcases Make A Wish's expanding multi-catalogue inventory—anchored by the newly integrated **Corporate Utility & Joining Kit Brochure (`H-Series`)**, the **PATRA Pure Copperware & Artisanal Brassware Collections (`PT-C` & `PT-B` Series)**, and the **Gourmet Hamper & Lifestyle Lines**—built on a **modular, extensible product data structure** so new product lines can be added continuously without redesigning the website.

## 1.2 Target Audience
- **HR & People Operations Teams:** Sourcing year-round **Employee Joining Kits** (5-in-1 / 6-in-1 combo sets, laptop backpacks, diaries, sipper bottles, tiffin boxes) and work-anniversary gifts.
- **Corporate Admin, IT & Procurement Managers:** Ordering bulk **New Year 2027 Diaries, Desktop Lifetime Calendars, Pens, Keychains, USB Pendrives, and Desk Stands**, or bundling **Corporate Event Decor + Bulk Gifting** under a single vendor.
- **CXOs, Marketing & Client Relations Heads:** Selecting high-prestige **PATRA Copperware & Brassware sets**, **999 Silver & German Silver keepsakes**, **Perfumes**, and **Luxury Gourmet Hampers** for VIP clients, board members, and trade-show delegates.

## 1.3 Primary Goal
Convert B2B visitors into qualified bulk inquiries through:
1. **Direct WhatsApp Inquiry Routing (`+91 97734 11805`)** with auto-populated SKU/model codes and quantity tiers.
2. **Structured Corporate Inquiry / RFQ Forms** routed directly to `makeawishevents@gmail.com` and a centralized lead sheet.

## 2.2 Future-Ready "Add More Products" Extensibility (`FR-02`)
- **Data-Driven Product Rendering:** All product cards, category tabs, SKU codes (`H934`, `PT-C-FTH-BTL-MT`, etc.), finishes/colors, and dimensions must be rendered dynamically from a single structured **Product Catalog Configuration (`catalog.json` or Google Sheet CSV feed)** rather than hardcoded HTML blocks.
- **Zero-Code Catalog Expansion:** Adding new brochures or product categories in the future requires only appending new objects to the catalog data file (with `sku`, `title`, `category`, `subCategory`, `specs`, `colors`, `dimensions`, and `imageUrl`), which automatically populates the category filter, search bar, and inquiry dropdowns.

## 2.3 Inquiry Form & Direct WhatsApp Lead Engine (`FR-03` & `FR-04`)
- **Corporate Inquiry Form:** Captures `Name`, `Company`, `Work Email`, `Phone/WhatsApp`, `Gifting Purpose` (*Employee Joining Kits*, *Conference/Event Giveaways*, *Client/Executive Gifting*, *New Year 2027 Diaries & Calendars*, *Turnkey Event Decor + Gifting Bundle*), `Selected SKUs / Categories`, and `Estimated Quantity Tier` (`25–49`, `50–99`, `100–249`, `250+`).

# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance:** Page load `< 2.5 seconds` on 4G/broadband; lazy-loaded `WebP` product thumbnails with pagination or "Load More" chunking so rendering 150+ SKUs across the 3 brochures never slows down initial page load.
- **Usability & Mobile Responsiveness:** Mobile-first grid (`360px` to `1440px+`), sticky category filter pills, instant SKU/keyword search (e.g., searching `"H989"`, `"Joining Kit"`, `"Copper"`, `"Diary"`, `"Backpack"`, or `"Dabara"`), and one-tap WhatsApp action buttons.
- **Security:** HTTPS enforced, Honeypot + Cloudflare Turnstile spam protection on inquiry forms, and zero exposure of internal wholesale rate cards in client-side source code.

# 4. Architecture & Why You Do (or Don't) Need a Backend Stack

## 4.1 Why Was a Backend Mentioned—And Do You Actually Need One?
Because your website has **no e-commerce checkout, no payment gateway, no public pricing, and no user logins**, **you do NOT need a traditional custom Backend Stack (like a Node.js/PHP server or SQL database) to launch or run this website.**

| Website Function | How It Works WITHOUT a Backend (Recommended Now) | Why Someone Might Add a Backend Later (Optional Phase 2) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. WhatsApp Inquiries** | **100% Frontend (`wa.me` link):** Browser JavaScript formats the selected SKU codes & quantities and opens WhatsApp directly. | Not needed for WhatsApp routing. |
| **2. Web Inquiry Form** | **Serverless Form Relay (Zero Backend Code):** Use **Web3Forms / Formspree** (free tier) or a **Google Apps Script Webhook** that sends the inquiry straight to `makeawishevents@gmail.com` and logs a row in a private **Google Sheet**. | Only needed if you want a custom internal CRM portal with staff logins. |
| **3. Adding New Products in the Future** | **JSON Catalog File or Google Sheet Sync:** Store products in a single `catalog.json` file (or fetch from a published Google Sheet). Add new rows anytime and the site updates automatically. | Only needed if non-technical staff want a drag-and-drop Admin Dashboard (CMS) to upload images. |

## 4.2 Recommended Architecture: Static Frontend + JSON/Google Sheet Catalog (Zero Backend Maintenance)
```
[Corporate Buyer Browser (Mobile / Desktop)]
       │
       ├──► 1. Static Frontend (HTML5 + Tailwind CSS + Vanilla JS / React)
       │         └── Hosted free/low-cost on Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or Hostinger
       │         └── Reads Product SKUs dynamically from `/data/catalog.json` (or Google Sheet)
       │
       ├──► 2. Direct WhatsApp CTA (`https://wa.me/919773411805?text=...`)
       │         └── Instant pre-filled B2B inquiry with SKU codes & quantity tier
       │
       └──► 3. Inquiry Form Submission
                 └── Web3Forms / Formspree / Google Apps Script Webhook
                       ├──► Instant Email Alert to `makeawishevents@gmail.com`
                       └──► Auto-logs Lead Row into Make A Wish Google Sheet Tracker
```

## 4.3 Extensible Product Schema (`catalog.json`) for Future Product Additions
Whenever you receive a new supplier brochure or product line, you simply add an entry following this lightweight structure:
```json
{
  "sku": "H989",
  "name": "5 in 1 Premium Corporate Joining Kit",
  "pillar": "joining-kits-combos",
  "category": "5 in 1 Combo Gift Sets",
  "inclusions": ["A5 Size Diary", "900ml Steel Water Bottle", "Metal Ball Pen", "Mobile Stand", "Office Laptop Bag"],
  "dimensions": "Standard Executive Kit",
  "colors": ["Black"],
  "occasionTags": ["Employee Onboarding", "Year-Round Corporate", "Conferences"],
  "image": "/images/catalog/h989-joining-kit.webp"
}
```

# 7. Exclusions (Out of Scope)

1. **No Public Pricing or Catalogue MRPs:** All Rupee prices (`₹` / `Rs.`) present in the PATRA Copper, PATRA Brass, and Hampers PDFs are strictly excluded from the website UI.
2. **No On-Website Product Customization:** Even though physical products in the brochure show `"Your Logo"` placement areas for B2B reference, the website will **not** offer interactive logo uploaders, custom box builders, or customization configurators.
3. **No E-Commerce Cart, Checkout, or Payment Gateway:** No online payments or retail single-piece checkout.
4. **No User Accounts or Heavy Backend Infrastructure:** No login portals or complex database servers to maintain.
